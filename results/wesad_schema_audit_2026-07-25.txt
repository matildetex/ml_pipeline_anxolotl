  CONCLUSION 1: thesis's "137 features" figure for WESAD-full matches ONLY the OLD, now-superseded schema. The CURRENT pipeline produces 130, missing RESP entirely and missing chest-ECG-derived HRV entirely (kept only wrist-derived HRV), and gained one channel (temp_chest) it did not have before.
