Three ablation studies — Anxolotl stress detection pipeline
========================================================================

FIXED CONFIGURATION
  Model        : LightGBM  n_estimators=300  max_depth=4  learning_rate=0.1
                 num_leaves=31  min_child_samples=10  class_weight=balanced
  CV           : LOSO (Leave-One-Subject-Out), n=37 subjects
  Window/stride: 60s / 30s (50% overlap) — Ablations 1 & 3
  BL-norm      : first N_BL windows per subject (chronological,
                 label-free proxy); N_BL fixed=8 unless varied
  Features     : 5 channels × 11 stats = 55 features total
    Channels   : acc_wrist, hr, ibi, rmssd, sdnn
    Stats      : mean, std, min, max, p25, p75, skew, kurtosis, slope, lf_hf, delta_mean

========================================================================
ABLATION 1 — N_BL sensitivity
  Dataset: 60s/30s, 699 windows, 37 subjects
  Variable: number of calibration windows used for BL-norm
  (All subjects have 9 baseline windows at 60s/30s; N_BL=8 leaves one out)

  N_BL    MacroF1      ±SD    AUROC   N_folds
--------------------------------------------
     4     0.7154   0.1318   0.8690        37
     6     0.8291   0.0981   0.9152        37
     8     0.8625   0.0996   0.9487        37

Interpretation: MacroF1 range across N_BL levels = 0.1471. N_BL=8 outperforms N_BL=4 by 0.1471 — calibration data quantity matters; more baseline windows improve normalization.

========================================================================
ABLATION 2 — Stride: 30s (50% overlap) vs 60s (no overlap)
  Window: 60s, BL-norm N_BL=8 (clamped to available baseline windows)
  Baseline windows/subject at stride=60s: 4–5 (N_BL clamps to this)

Config                         N_win    N_BL    N_ST  Win/subj    MacroF1      ±SD    AUROC
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
stride=30s (50% overlap)         699     334     365      18.9     0.8625   0.0996   0.9487
stride=60s (no overlap)          378     185     193      10.2     0.6773   0.1466   0.7679

Interpretation: stride=30s vs stride=60s delta = +0.1852. stride=30s (50% overlap) scores higher. This may reflect genuine benefit of more training data OR mild optimistic bias from correlated overlapping windows in the training set.

ABLATION 3 — Marker-confirmed vs marker-inferred
  Dataset: 60s/30s, 699 windows, 37 subjects
  BL-norm N_BL=8. Training set = all 37 subjects; test subset varies.
  Confirmed: subjects where all 8 protocol markers were gesture-detected
  Inferred : subjects where <8 markers — at least one phase boundary
             was inferred from timing or is absent

  Marker count per subject:
    n_markers=4:  2 subjects  [p32, p34]
    n_markers=5: 11 subjects  [p15, p19, p20, p21, p25, p28, p37, p39, p44, p8, p9]
    n_markers=6: 20 subjects  [p10, p11, p13, p14, p16, p17, p18, p23, p26, p27, p29, p30, p33, p35, p38, p40, p41, p42, p43, p7]
    n_markers=7:  3 subjects  [p12, p31, p36]
    n_markers=8:  1 subjects  [p22]

Group                            N_subj  N_testwin    MacroF1      ±SD    AUROC   N_folds
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Confirmed (n_markers=8)               1         25     0.9167   0.0000   0.9867         1
Inferred  (n_markers<8)              36        674     0.8610   0.1006   0.9477        36

Interpretation: confirmed (1 subjects) vs inferred (36 subjects) delta = +0.0556. Confirmed sessions score higher. Sessions with inferred boundaries may contain mislabelled windows, inflating difficulty. Reporting the confirmed-session F1 is the conservative choice.
