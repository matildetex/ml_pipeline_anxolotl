====================================================================================================
CORRECTION (appended 2026-07-26, after review) -- lf_hf should NOT be grouped with slope/delta_mean
====================================================================================================
The "aggressive" tier's inclusion of lf_hf was conceptually flawed. lf_hf IS
order-dependent (Welch PSD requires the sample sequence to define frequency
content -- permuting samples destroys it), but order-dependence is not the
same property as "measures within-window trend/drift". slope and delta_mean
are trend statistics by direct construction (linear fit vs. time index;
second-half-minus-first-half). lf_hf measures spectral/oscillatory balance
(sympathovagal ratio) -- a real physiological quantity, not a drift artifact
-- and has no established interpretation as a "temporal trend" feature. The
aggressive-tier number above (40 feat, Macro F1=0.9053, delta=+0.0115,
p=0.056) is NOT part of the thesis-facing argument for this reason -- see
results/section_1_5_1_temporal_confounding_draft_2026-07-26.txt for the
corrected write-up, which relies only on the conservative tier (slope,
delta_mean) plus the confirmation-check finding (0/15 a-priori features in
the real top-15), neither of which depends on this correction.
