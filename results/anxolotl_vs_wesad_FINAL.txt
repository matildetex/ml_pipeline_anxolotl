NOTE ON AUROC — deliberately dropped from the table above.
Per-fold AUROC saturates to ~1.0 in almost every LOSO fold at 90s (36-37 of 37),
INCLUDING THE WATCH-ONLY CONFIG WITH NO ECG AT ALL (30/37 folds already at
F1=1.0, AUC=1.0 in 37/37). This is not ECG-driven and not leakage — verified by
checking the same BL-norm methodology at 60s, where it does NOT saturate
(AUC~0.96-0.99). AUROC only requires correctly ranking pairs within a fold, and
at 90s each fold has just 7-14 test windows — correct ranking of a handful of
pairs is a much easier bar than getting F1=1.0 at a fixed 0.5 threshold, and is
not evidence of production-grade reliability. MacroF1 remains the metric that
still shows real, meaningful spread across subjects (sd 0.045-0.104, with
p33/p38/p20 consistently harder than the rest) and is what should be reported
and discussed, not the saturated AUROC.

KEY FINDINGS
1. Anxolotl watch-only (60s, no ECG) already beats WESAD-all (which DOES include
   chest ECG): 0.918 vs 0.872. The Anxolotl advantage is not about sensors — it's
   the gesture-marked protocol giving a cleaner baseline/stress boundary than
   WESAD's TSST / driving-simulation protocol.
2. ECG adds a real, consistent gain on the Anxolotl side at both windows tested:
   +0.032 at 60s (0.918 -> 0.950), +0.017 at 90s (0.969 -> 0.986). Never hurts,
   across 9 different model types.
3. ECG also cuts fold-to-fold variance sharply at 90s (sd 0.104 -> 0.045) —
   not just a higher mean, a more STABLE model across subjects.
4. Best defensible number overall: Anxolotl Watch+ECG @ 90s, MacroF1 = 0.9857.
   This is +0.064 above the SOTA reference (~0.921), and +0.113 / +0.134 above
   WESAD's own wrist / all-channel numbers.

CAVEATS
- 90s is the largest window confirmed to have a reliable per-fold sample size
  (>=7 windows/subject); 120s/150s were tested and rejected earlier for the
  watch-only case (near-perfect scores at those sizes are a small-sample
  artifact, not evidence of a better model) — the same caution partially
  applies at 90s too (see AUROC note above), though MacroF1 still shows real
  differential difficulty across subjects, unlike at 120s/150s.
- ECG @ 90s used a freshly re-extracted feature set (ecg_biopac_features_90s.py)
  since the original processed_data_anxolotl_ecg.pkl was only built at 60s;
  window boundaries were verified to match the watch tabular pipeline exactly
  (no window-count mismatches across all 36 subjects with BIOPAC data).
- p41 has no BIOPAC recording in either ECG extraction (excluded / NaN, imputed).
