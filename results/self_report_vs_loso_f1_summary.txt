INTERPRETATION FOR THE THESIS / DEFENSE:
No credible evidence that subjects who *felt* more stressed were easier (or
harder) for the model to classify. This should be framed as an honest,
reported-not-hidden null result: physiological stress detection and
subjective stress perception are, at minimum, not strongly coupled at the
per-subject level in this dataset — consistent with the broader
psychophysiology literature on the dissociation between self-report and
autonomic arousal. It is not evidence against the model (which is evaluated
against the experimental protocol's phase labels, not against self-report),
but it does mean self-report cannot be offered as external validation of
per-subject model difficulty.
